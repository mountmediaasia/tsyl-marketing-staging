Product Spotlight

WDS 1700
Air Duct Sealant

A solvent-based sealant suitable for sealing joints or gaps up to 1.5 mm wide or 1.5 mm in diameter. Recommended for adhesion to metals such as zinc sheet and galvanised sheets.

Good initial grab; does not slump or sag when applied no more than 2 mm thick. Remains soft and flexible without cracking.

Application

  1. Ensure surfaces to be bonded are clean, sound, dry and free from dust and oil.
  2. Apply onto the seam.

Highly flammable solvent; fire retardant when dried.
