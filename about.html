Overview

A leading supplier of insulation, fire safety & building materials.

TSYL Marketing Sdn. Bhd. is a leading trading company providing a comprehensive range of insulation products and solutions for air-conditioning, building materials, and fire safety applications. Established in December 2009, we have built our reputation on product quality, supply reliability, and technical know-how.

We deliver effective insulation and sealant solutions to industrial and domestic sectors — including HVAC ductwork insulation, construction structural sealants, automotive insulation, factory ducting, and complete passive fire-proofing systems.

Headquartered in Kuala Lumpur with a branch office in Kuching, Sarawak, our nationwide reach combines prompt delivery, consistent stock, and competitive pricing for clients of every scale.

Our Journey

From a Kepong warehouse
to a nationwide supply network.

2009

Founded in Kuala Lumpur

TSYL is incorporated in December 2009, beginning operations as a specialist trading company for air-conditioning insulation materials.

2011

First major infrastructure supply

Begins supplying insulation to large-scale infrastructure works, building the foundation of a portfolio that would grow to 400+ projects.

2014

MRT Line 1 Klang Valley supply

Selected as a supplier for the Klang Valley MRT Line 1 — TSYL's first contribution to Malaysia's national rail infrastructure.

2018

CIDB & ISO 9001 certified

Achievement of CIDB and ISO 9001-2008 certifications, formalising our commitment to quality management and construction industry standards.

2020

Kuching branch opens

Expansion into East Malaysia with a branch in Kuching, Sarawak — enabling faster delivery for projects across Borneo.

2023

Merdeka 118 & TRX delivery

TSYL supplies insulation and fire safety systems to two of Malaysia's most prestigious developments — Merdeka 118 and Tun Razak Exchange.

2026

Building the next decade

Today, TSYL Marketing continues to supply 300+ clients with a complete catalogue of thermal insulation, passive fire systems, sealants, and GBI-aligned solutions.
