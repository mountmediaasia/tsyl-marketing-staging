Company Overview

Your partner in
building performance.

TSYL Marketing Sdn. Bhd. supplies air-conditioning insulation materials, HVAC accessories and building supplies across Malaysia. Drawing on the TSYL brand’s experience since 2009, we help businesses source materials for efficient, safe building systems.

Our range supports commercial, industrial and residential HVAC applications, with a focus on thermal performance, energy efficiency and fire safety. We combine quality materials with practical product knowledge and reliable service.

From our Kuala Lumpur head office and Kuching branch, we serve customers nationwide with consistent stock availability, prompt delivery and competitive pricing.
