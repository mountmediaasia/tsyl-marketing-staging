Company Overview

Your partner in
building performance.

TSYL Marketing Sdn. Bhd. is a trusted trading company specializing in air-conditioning insulation materials, accessories, and building supplies. With a strong foundation established in 2009, our company continues to build upon years of experience and technical expertise gained through the TSYL brand.

We are committed to providing high-performance, energy-efficient, and safety-focused insulation solutions for HVAC systems used across commercial, industrial, and residential projects. Our dedication to quality, service reliability, and innovation positions us as a preferred supplier within Malaysia’s construction and mechanical industries.

Headquartered in Kuala Lumpur with a branch office in Kuching, Sarawak, we serve clients nationwide with prompt delivery, consistent stock availability, and competitive pricing.
