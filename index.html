About TSYL Vision

Built on three decades
of insulation expertise.

TSYL Vision Sdn. Bhd. is a leading supplier of insulation products and solutions for air-conditioning and building applications. With over 30 years of experience embedded in our leadership, we specialise in fire-rated duct systems, firestop solutions, flexible connector canvases, and complete HVAC insulation services.

Headquartered in Kuala Lumpur with a branch office in Kuching, Sarawak, we safeguard mission-critical environments — protecting both people and infrastructure with solutions that meet international standards.
